🏫 About Madarsa Ittehad -E- Millat, Bhantha

Madarsa Ittehad -E- Millat, Bhantha (Darbhanga, Bihar) is dedicated to providing Islamic and moral education to underprivileged children. Many families in rural Bihar cannot afford proper education, and madarsas become the only source of learning and guidance.

Your donation helps build classrooms, provide study materials, and support students in gaining knowledge of the Quran, Hadith, and basic education.

📖 Importance of Madarsa Education

  • ✔ Quran and Islamic teachings
  • ✔ Moral and ethical values
  • ✔ Discipline and character building
  • ✔ Basic modern education

Madarsa education shapes a strong and responsible generation.

🤲 Earn Sadaqah Jariyah

Sadaqah Jariyah is ongoing charity that continues to reward you even after your lifetime. By donating to a Madarsa:

  • 📖 Every Quran recited gives reward
  • 🕌 Every prayer taught gives reward
  • 👨‍🎓 Every student educated gives reward

This is a chance to earn continuous blessings.
